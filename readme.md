# Tap Tunes 🎵

A rhythm game inspired by OSU where timing and precision meet musical mastery.

## Overview

**Tap Tunes** is a rhythm-based game where players aim to achieve the highest score possible by timing their inputs perfectly with the beat of the song. Test your reflexes, challenge yourself with debuffs, and climb the leaderboards across multiple maps!

## Gameplay

### Core Mechanics

Players interact with three types of game components that appear in sync with the music:

- **Tap Circles** - Click on these circles precisely on beat to score points
- **Sliders** - Hold and drag your mouse within the bounds for multiple beats
- **Spinners** - Rotate as fast as possible within the time limit to maximize points

### Scoring System

Your score is calculated based on:
- **Timing accuracy** of each input (Perfect, Great, Good, Miss)
- **Combo multiplier** - Chain successful hits to increase your score
- **Active debuffs** - Risk vs reward for even higher scores

### Debuffs (Hard Mode)

Increase the challenge and score multiplier by activating debuffs:
- ⚡ Increased song speed
- 👁️ Limited field of view
- And more...

## Game Features

### Screens

- **Menu Screen** - Navigate to different game modes
- **Tutorial** - Learn the mechanics with interactive examples
- **Map Select** - Browse available songs with difficulty ratings and previews
- **Options** - Customize your gameplay experience
- **Credits** - Meet the team behind Tap Tunes

### Multiple Maps

Each map features unique songs with varying difficulty levels to challenge players of all skill ranges.

## How to Play

1. Launch the game and select a map
2. Click on tap circles when they align
3. Hold and follow sliders along their path
4. Spin the spinners as fast as possible
5. Chain combos for higher scores
6. Activate debuffs for extra challenge and points!

**Note:** This is a student project created for educational purposes and is inspired by OSU.
